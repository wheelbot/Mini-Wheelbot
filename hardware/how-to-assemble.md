# Assembling the Mini Wheelbot

## Motors
To assemble the motors, first the precrimped JST-XH (2 in) cables are cut to length, soldered to the motors and inserted into the JST-XH 3 pin plug housings.

## Motor connector cable

The precrimped JST-XH (4 in) cables are cut to length, soldered to the PCB and inserted into the JST-XH 3 pin plug housing. The cables are shielded using shielding copper tape.
